Logging in

Use your Admission Number or your Full Name exactly as registered. New student? Tap Register as New Student on the login page.

What you can do

  • My Profile – see/edit your details, print your ID card or PDF.
  • My Courses / Course Registration – see your courses and register them each semester.
  • My Results & GPA – only results your lecturers have published.
  • Attendance & Timetable – your own records and class schedule.
  • Announcements & Notifications – school messages for you or your department.
  • Support – send a ticket to the school and read the reply.
  • My Documents – admission letter, registration slip, result slip, transcript, ID card.
  • Departmental Papers – view/download ND1 & ND2 papers.

Install the app

Open Settings → Install App (or your browser menu → Install / Add to Home screen).
